**Our Project:**  
PriVis

**Our Slogan:**  
“*See the access. Reduce the risk.*”

**Our Purpose:**  
PriVis is a cybersecurity application designed to help small organizations identify excessive, outdated, and potentially inappropriate employee access permissions. By comparing employee roles against existing access privileges, PriVis highlights potential security risks and helps organizations make informed decisions based on the principle of least privilege.

**Product name:** PriVis

**Name meaning:** Privilege \+ Visibility

**Product category:** Identity and Access Management (IAM) visibility and security analytics

**Primary audience:** Small businesses, IT administrators, cybersecurity analysts, and organizations managing employee access.

**Product positioning:** A third-party visibility and analysis tool that works alongside existing directory systems such as Microsoft Active Directory, rather than replacing them.

**Core capabilities:** Understand user privileges, identify excessive or outdated access, visualize group memberships, explain security findings, and simulate potential permission changes.
